PI DRAWER 6 MM MAGNET GLUE-FIT REPLACEMENTS
============================================

These replace both Pi drawers only:

  05_Pi_Drawer_1_6mm_Magnet_Fit.stl
  06_Pi_Drawer_2_6mm_Magnet_Fit.stl

For Bambu A1 printing, open:

  homelab-rack-Pi-Drawers-6mm-Magnet-Fit.3mf

The chassis and vent remain unchanged. Do not reprint them for this correction.
The drawer envelopes, labels, rails, detents, board locators, magnetic gap,
screw-head clearance, and every chassis interface remain unchanged.

Each drawer has four revised underside magnet pockets:

  Straight glue-fit bore:       6.60 mm
  Underside bed-face mouth:     approximately 7.00 mm
  Support-free entrance flare:  0.70 mm high
  Magnet:                       nominal 6 x 2 mm
  Pocket depth:                 unchanged at 4.55 mm
  Insulating roof/skin:         unchanged at 0.30 mm
  Local boss outside diameter:  8.60 mm
  Nominal radial boss wall:     1.00 mm

The 7.0 mm flared entrance prevents first-layer elephant foot from reducing the
usable 6.6 mm bore. The boss diameter increases only around the four mounting
pockets; its height and mounting centers are unchanged.

MAGNET INSTALLATION

- These are glue-fit pockets, not press-fit pockets. Do not force the magnets.
- Confirm polarity against the chassis magnets before applying adhesive.
- Use a small amount of two-part epoxy or thick cyanoacrylate around the magnet
  edge. Keep adhesive away from the center of the insulating roof.
- Slide the magnet through the flared underside entrance and hold it gently
  against the pocket roof until the adhesive sets.
- Do not drill through or scrape the 0.30 mm insulating roof.

The existing pocket depth intentionally leaves room below a 2 mm magnet so it
can be inserted and glued against the roof. The replacement drawers are the
first article; no separate gauge or test plate is required.
