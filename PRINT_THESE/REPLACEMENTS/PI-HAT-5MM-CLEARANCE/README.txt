PI HAT +5 MM CLEARANCE REPLACEMENT
==================================

Print this replacement instead of 04_Dual_Pi_Chassis.stl:

  homelab-rack-Dual-Pi-Chassis-5mm-HAT-Clearance.3mf

The 3MF is the recommended Bambu A1 project. It contains one chassis and two
small build-plate-only manual support strips beneath the raised front top rails.
Remove both strips through the two open Pi bay fronts after printing.

Do not reprint either Pi drawer or the vent cartridge. The existing printed
drawers, magnetic holders, detents, guide lips, rails, and vent cartridge remain
compatible and unchanged.

Opening dimensions:

  Left Pi bay:   62 x 35 mm, bottom Z = 6 mm
  Center vent:   62 x 30 mm, bottom Z = 6 mm
  Right Pi bay:  62 x 35 mm, bottom Z = 6 mm

The outer openings extend exactly 5 mm higher than the original 30 mm openings.
The front cut continues through the existing 34 mm clearance depth and then
reaches the chassis's already-open interior. The original 30 mm drawer
faceplates intentionally leave a 5 mm headroom slot above each drawer.

The remaining front top rail is 2.7 mm high. The no-support A/B slice exposed
61.6 mm overhang-wall paths beneath these two rails, so the recommended 3MF
uses localized supports there only. The supports do not enter the detent
pockets, guide lips, drawer rails, center vent bay, magnetic holder geometry,
rack ears, stacking features, or open chassis interior.

No cosmetic eyebrow/filler is included because any clip occupying the 5 mm slot
would reduce the required coil insertion clearance.

If using either existing five-plate multi-plate project:

1. Open Plate 3.
2. Disable or delete only 04_Dual_Pi_Chassis.stl.
3. Leave 07_Vent_Insert.stl enabled if it has not already been printed.
4. Print the standalone replacement 3MF above.

If the vent is already printed, print only the standalone replacement project.

Pinned targeted-support estimate:

  113.22 g PLA
  4h50m34s

The no-support comparison was 107.12 g and 4h36m37s. The localized support
cost is 6.10 g and 13m57s.
